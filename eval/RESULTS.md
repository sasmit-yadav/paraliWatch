# Forecast results

Generated by `python -m parali train`. Do not edit by hand.

**Task:** for every H3 cell (~36 km2) in Punjab and Haryana and every day, predict whether NASA VIIRS will detect at least one fire in that cell in the next 3 days.

**Split:** train 2020-2023, validation (early stopping, calibration) 2024, test 2025 (used once, for this report).

## Test season 2025

Fire capture @10% = share of all fires in the next 3 days that fell inside the 10% of cells the method ranked highest that day. It answers: if officials could only watch 1 in 10 cells, how many fires would they cover?

| Method | PR-AUC | ROC-AUC | Precision @10% | Fire capture @10% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LightGBM model (raw scores, used for ranking)** | **0.266** | **0.849** | **16.2%** | **36.8%** |
| Baseline: Fires in the cell, last 3 days | 0.107 | 0.602 | 11.1% | 25.7% |
| Baseline: Fires in and around the cell, last 3 days | 0.191 | 0.748 | 14.6% | 33.6% |
| Baseline: Fires in the same cell, same days last year | 0.093 | 0.608 | 10.8% | 23.3% |
| Baseline: Cell's average fires per season (earlier years) | 0.084 | 0.692 | 9.1% | 20.6% |

Base rate (share of cell-days with a fire in the next 3 days): 4.9%. A random ranking would score PR-AUC equal to the base rate.
